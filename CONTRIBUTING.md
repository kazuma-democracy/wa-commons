# Contributing to WA Commons

Thank you for considering a contribution.

WA Commons welcomes disagreement, replication, criticism, and negative results. A contributor does not need to share a single political ideology; contributors do need to accept transparent evidence, non-coercion, and good-faith review.

## Ways to contribute

### Engineering
- connectors and ingestion;
- entity resolution;
- provenance storage;
- agent workflows;
- testing and reproducibility;
- privacy/security;
- frontend and accessibility.

### Research
- public dataset discovery;
- evidence taxonomy;
- company ownership structures;
- portfolio methodology;
- measurement of peace-positive externalities;
- comparison with existing products and OSS.

### Review
- false-positive hunting;
- ideological/bias red-teaming;
- legal/compliance review;
- source-quality challenges;
- adversarial examples.

### Communication
- Japanese/English translation;
- documentation;
- diagrams;
- public experiment diary;
- onboarding.

## Contribution rules

1. Claims about a named organization should include sources.
2. Separate facts, inference, and value judgments.
3. Prefer primary sources when available.
4. Never infer sensitive traits about individuals.
5. Do not submit covert persuasion, impersonation, harassment, or mass-targeting features.
6. Financial execution is out of scope until research and paper-trading controls are mature.
7. A finding may be `UNKNOWN` or `DISPUTED`; forcing a binary answer is not a goal.
8. Document the license and permitted use of every imported dataset.

## Suggested first contributions

- Audit the proposed evidence schema.
- Identify 5 high-quality public data sources and their licenses.
- Compare existing entity-resolution OSS.
- Design an evidence-card JSON schema.
- Design a reproducible paper-portfolio evaluation.
- Translate the manifesto into another language.
