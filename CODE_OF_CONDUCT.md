# WA Commons Code of Conduct

WA Commons is a public-interest engineering experiment. Strong disagreement is welcome; harassment is not.

## Expected

- Critique claims and mechanisms rather than attacking people.
- Cite evidence when making factual accusations about organizations.
- State uncertainty and conflicts of interest.
- Welcome correction and reproducible counter-evidence.
- Protect personal and sensitive information.
- Make room for dissenting and minority views.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, doxxing, or intimidation.
- Impersonation or covert influence operations.
- Coordinated targeting of private individuals.
- Knowingly false evidence or manipulated provenance.
- Attempts to convert the project into a tool for political retaliation.

Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and the integrity of the project. Governance and enforcement procedures should become more formal as the community grows.
