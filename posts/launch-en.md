# Introducing WA Commons — an open experiment in peace-positive technology

Can software make peaceful choices structurally easier and more rewarding?

WA Commons is a new open-source experiment from Japan.

“WA” can mean harmony and peace (和), a circle of connection (輪), an ecosystem or cycle (環), and dialogue (話).

Our premise is simple: peace should not depend on goodwill alone. A person should be able to use a service because it saves time, improves a decision, or creates economic value — while the architecture quietly routes a little more money, opportunity, and attention toward choices consistent with that person's peace-related values.

Our first experiment is **Peace Capital**: an evidence-driven research tool for value-aligned investing that still takes ordinary financial goals seriously.

We are publishing before we have the answer. We will share the reasoning, discarded ideas, OSS we reuse, failures, disputed classifications, and design changes.

We welcome engineers, researchers, finance practitioners, lawyers, designers, translators, security reviewers, skeptics, and people willing to challenge the project’s assumptions.

We are not trying to build an AI that decides what is morally correct for everyone.

We want to build weakly empowered but extremely persistent public-interest agents: transparent, auditable tools that keep checking evidence and doing small useful work long after human attention moves on.

From Japan, for anyone who wants to build and test peace technology with us.
